BUSINESS PURPOSE AND SCALE

Zahra Plast distributes plastic household, school, kitchen, storage, and related
products from multiple brands to customers with individually agreed pricing.
Zehra Plus is a custom web application for customer-specific discounts, quotations,
invoices, controlled invoice exceptions, pricing history, and management reports.

Initial catalog: 317 products, 309 numeric rates, 8 brands, and 15 categories.
Initial customer target: 150, expandable. No customer list or existing discount
agreements were supplied in these two PDFs. The 47,550 figure is 150 x 317 possible
customer-product combinations, not evidence of 47,550 existing agreements or a
hard technical ceiling. Products and suppliers must support further additions.
Single currency: PKR, explicitly stated in scope section 12, page 5.

Brands: SAFARI, Plasti Ware, Kiku, Novel, Aqua, Cosmic, Royal, Desire.
Categories: School Bottle, Water Bottles, Jar, Lunch Box, Salt and Pepper,
Sugar Pot, Storage Box, Kitchen Item, Bowl Set, House Hold, Cabinet, Glass, Jug,

SCOPE PAGE 1 -- OBJECTIVES AND PRODUCT MASTER

Business objectives:
- Automatically apply the correct customer-specific price or discount.
- Maintain thousands of customer-product rules efficiently through bulk tools.
- Allow per-line or whole-invoice discount adjustments without changing masters.
- Keep the full history of discount changes and invoice overrides.
- Show management which discounts differ from agreed terms.

Product fields: unique product code, name, brand/supplier, category, list rate,
packing, carton quantity, status, rate effective date, optional cost price, and
optional product image for invoices/catalogs. Status choices are Active,
Coming Soon, and Inactive.

Crucial unit rule: the rate prices one SELLING UNIT. A pack of 2 is one product
at one rate, not two units each charged that rate. Product names alone cannot
determine packing. A set described as 7 pieces may have PACKING = 1pc, meaning
one selling set. Cabinet packing may describe drawer/component count. Preserve
raw packing and distinguish it from carton quantity and invoice quantity.

The source's S.No is a catalog row reference. It is not a separately documented,
stable business SKU. Unique product, supplier, and customer codes must be agreed
or assigned during migration, with traceability back to source rows.

SCOPE PAGE 2 -- MASTERS, RATE VERSIONS, AND DISCOUNT RULES

Supplier master: code, name, contact details, terms; no fixed supplier-count limit.
Customer master: code, name, contact details, address, payment terms, optional group.
Each rate list has an effective date. Importing a new rate list does not change
saved customer discounts. Existing invoices keep their issued rates and discounts.

Two pricing-rule types:
1. Percentage: deduct a percentage from the list rate. Source example: rate 200,
   discount 10%, resulting net unit price 180.
2. Fixed net price: an agreed selling price per unit regardless of list rate.
   Source example: list rate 200, fixed net price 175. This is not an amount-off rule.

Each discount record has effective-from, optional effective-to, and active/inactive
status. Rule priority, from most specific to least specific:
1. Customer + Product.
2. Customer + Brand OR Customer + Category.
3. Customer-wide default.
4. No discount: use list rate.
The document does not resolve a simultaneous brand-versus-category match.

Bulk functions: full matrix Excel/CSV import and export; mass update such as
"+2% on all Kiku products for Customer X"; customer-to-customer copy; customer-group
to-member copy. Group copying is specified; live group inheritance is not.

Import validation rejects unknown customer/product codes, duplicates, negative
percentages, percentages over 100, fixed prices at or below zero, and overlapping
dates. Invalid rows need clear errors. Bulk-update percentage-point semantics,
fixed-price handling, and copy overwrite behavior still need definition.

Only Pricing Manager or Admin permission can change the discount matrix. Admin can
enable/configure an optional approval step. The audit log records who changed what,
when, and old/new values. An optional workflow is not evidence that it is enabled.

SCOPE PAGES 2-3 -- ALL 13 INVOICE OVERRIDE REQUIREMENTS

Central invariant: invoice-only adjustments do not silently update the saved
customer pricing rules. Saving or posting an invoice must leave the matrix intact.

1. Adding a product fills in its percentage discount or fixed net price from the
   applicable rule hierarchy.
2. Before confirmation, authorized users can change one line or apply one discount
   to all lines. Percentage and fixed net price inputs are supported.
3. There is no stated maximum override limit. Admin grants override permission to
   selected users or roles. Do not invent a business cap.
4. Store both the original matrix discount and the applied discount on each line.
5. Label each line "Matrix" or "Manual override".
6. Provide "Reset to matrix discount" for one line or the whole invoice.
7. Saving/posting never updates the matrix; matrix maintenance belongs in Discount
   Management with its separate permissions.
8. Override reason supports a selectable list and free text. Admin can make it
   mandatory. The exact default is not stated.
9. Posted invoice discounts are locked. Corrections use a credit note or controlled
   reversal, not direct editing of posted discounts.
10. Later matrix changes do not change existing invoices. Drafts keep their values
    until an explicit reset to matrix.
11. Quote-to-invoice conversion carries the quote discount and its override label.
12. Credit notes and returns use the original invoice discount.
13. Optional "Save to matrix" is OFF by default and restricted to Pricing Manager.
    If introduced, this deliberate operation must be distinguished from ordinary
    invoice saving, and reconciled with the Discount Management-only wording.

The no-limit rule is a permission policy; the document does not fully define the
valid numeric domain for invoice overrides. Do not automatically infer that
negative prices or over-100% discounts are accepted. Matrix validation is explicit;
override numeric validation remains a discovery item.

SCOPE PAGE 3 -- INVOICING, REPORTS, ROLES, AND TECHNICAL APPROACH

Each invoice line displays list rate, discount (percentage or fixed net price),
discount amount, net price, and line total. Show packing and carton quantity;
support orders in cartons. Coming Soon and Inactive products cannot be invoiced.
Implementation implication: carton ordering will need defined unit-conversion
rules; the source does not specify their arithmetic. It does not define a full
warehouse/inventory/procurement system.

Required reports:
- Customer discount sheet for all products and discounts of one customer.
- Product comparison showing one product's discount across customers.
- Supplier/brand discount summary.
- Missing-discount report for combinations without a defined rule.
- Discount change log.
- Invoice Override Report with invoice, customer, product, original matrix
  discount, applied discount, difference, user, reason, date.
- Override frequency by user, customer, product.
- Effect of overrides on invoice value.

Roles:
- Admin: configuration, users, permissions; matrix-change permission per section 3.4.
- Pricing Manager: create, edit, approve matrix discounts; view override reports.
- Sales / Billing User: create quotes and invoices; override only when permitted.
- Finance: view-only invoices, discount reports, audit logs.
Whether a Pricing Manager can approve their own change is unspecified. Credit-note
and reversal permissions are not separately assigned in the role table.

Technical requirements: custom web application, central database, secure login,
role-based access, audit trail for discount and invoice changes. No framework,
database product, hosting provider, or authentication vendor is selected.
