 1. Introduction and Objective
 Zahra Plast distributes plastic household and school products from multiple suppliers and sells to a large
 number of customers, each with their own agreed pricing. This document defines the scope of work for a
 custom-built system that stores a separate discount for every customer on every product, applies it
 automatically on quotes and invoices, and allows a controlled, one-time change of discount on an individual
 invoice without affecting the saved customer discounts.

 1.1 Business objectives
    Apply the correct customer-specific price or discount automatically at invoicing.
    Maintain discounts efficiently in bulk, even with thousands of customer-product combinations.
    Allow users to adjust discounts on an invoice, per line or for the whole invoice, without changing the master
     discount records.
    Keep a complete history of every discount change and every invoice-level override.
    Provide management with clear visibility of discounts given and where they differ from agreed terms.

 2. Master Data Setup
 2.1 Product master
 Loaded from the Zahra Plast rate list. Fields: product code, name, brand/supplier, category, rate (list price),
 packing (1 pc, 2 pcs, 3 pcs, 6 pcs, etc.), carton quantity (e.g. 96 pkt), status (Active / Coming Soon / Inactive),
 rate effective date and an optional cost price field.
    Categories: School Bottle, Water Bottles, Jar, Lunch Box, Salt and Pepper, Sugar Pot, Storage Box, Kitchen
     Item, Bowl Set, House Hold, Cabinet, Glass, Jug, Ice Box and Cooler.
    The rate is the price of one selling unit; for example, a "pack of 2" is one product at one rate.
    Each product has a unique code, as several product names are similar across brands.
    Product images are optional and can be attached if required for invoices or catalogues.

 2.2 Supplier and customer masters
    Suppliers/brands: code, name, contact details and terms. The number of suppliers is not limited.
    Customers: code, name, contact details, address, payment terms and an optional customer group.

 2.3 Rate list versioning
    Each rate list is stored with an effective date. The 20 October 2026 list is Version 1.
    New rate lists can be imported in future; customer discounts remain unchanged.
    Existing invoices always keep the rate and discount they were issued with.

 3. Discount Management
 3.1 Discount types
 Each customer-product discount can be defined in either of two ways:

 Type                       Description                                                   Example

 Percentage                 A percentage deducted from the list rate                      Rate 200, discount 10%, net price 180

 Fixed net price            A special agreed selling price per unit, regardless of        Rate 200, fixed net price 175
                            list rate

 Each discount record also holds an effective-from date, an optional effective-to date and an active/inactive
 status.

 3.2 Discount hierarchy
 When an invoice is created, the system selects the most specific applicable rule:

 Priority             Rule

 1                    Customer + Product

 2                    Customer + Brand or Category

 3                    Customer-wide default

 4                    No discount (list rate)

 3.3 Bulk tools
    Import and export of the complete discount matrix in Excel/CSV format.
    Mass update, for example "+2% on all Kiku products for Customer X".
    Copy discounts from one customer to another, or from a customer group to its members.
    Import validation: unknown customer or product codes, duplicates, negative or over-100% values, fixed
     prices at or below zero, and overlapping dates. Invalid rows are rejected with clear error messages.

 3.4 Approval and audit
    Changes to the discount matrix are made only by users with the Pricing Manager or Admin permission.
    An optional approval step for matrix changes can be enabled and configured by the Admin.
    A complete audit log records who changed what, when, and the old and new values.

 4. One-Time Discount Change on Invoice
    Core rule: a discount changed on an invoice applies to that invoice only. It is never written back
    to the customer-product discount matrix.


 #        Requirement

 1        When a product is added to an invoice, the system fills in the discount or fixed net price from the matrix using the
          hierarchy in section 3.2.

 2        Before the invoice is confirmed, an authorized user can change the discount on any single line, or apply one
          discount to the whole invoice (all lines at once). Users can enter either a percentage or a fixed net price.

 #        Requirement

 3        There is no maximum limit on the override. Permission to override is granted by the Admin to specific users or roles.

 4        The system stores two values on each line: the original matrix discount and the applied discount.

 5        Each line is labelled "Matrix" or "Manual override".

 6        A "Reset to matrix discount" button restores the saved value on a line or on the whole invoice.

 7        Saving or posting an invoice never updates the matrix. The matrix can only be changed in the Discount Management
          module by users with that permission.

 8        A reason can be recorded for each override (selectable list plus free text). The Admin can configure whether the
          reason is mandatory.

 9        Once an invoice is posted, its discounts are locked. Corrections are made by credit note or controlled reversal.

 10       Later changes to the matrix do not alter existing invoices. Draft invoices keep their values unless the user selects
          "Reset to matrix".

 11       When a quote is converted to an invoice, the quote's discount is carried over, including the override label.

 12       Credit notes and returns use the discount from the original invoice.

 13       Optional, switched off by default: a "Save to matrix" action restricted to the Pricing Manager, so the matrix is never
          updated by accident.

 4.1 Override reporting
    Invoice Override Report: invoice, customer, product, matrix discount, applied discount, difference, user,
     reason and date.
    Override frequency by user, customer and product, to highlight where the matrix may need a permanent
     update.
    Discount impact of overrides on invoice value.

 5. Invoicing and Reporting
 5.1 Invoice presentation
    Each line shows list rate, discount (percentage or fixed net price), discount amount, net price and line total.
    Packing size and carton quantity are shown; ordering in cartons is supported.
    Products with "Coming Soon" or inactive status cannot be invoiced.

 5.2 Reports
    Customer discount sheet: all products and discounts for one customer.
    Product comparison: one product's discount across all customers.
    Supplier/brand-wise discount summary.
    Missing-discount report: customer-product combinations with no rule defined.
    Discount change log and invoice override reports (section 4.1).

 5.3 User roles

 Role                                    Access

 Admin                                   System configuration, user and permission management

 Pricing Manager                         Create, edit and approve matrix discounts; view override reports

 Sales / Billing User                    Create quotes and invoices; override discounts where permission is granted

 Finance                                 View-only access to invoices, discount reports and audit logs

 6. Technical Approach
    The solution is a custom-built, web-based application with a central database and secure user login.
    Role-based access control, with all discount and invoice changes recorded in an audit trail.

 11. Responsibilities
    Client: provide customer list, current discount agreements and corrected rate list; nominate a decision-maker
     for pricing rules; review and sign off each phase; take part in user acceptance testing; give feedback within 2
     business days.
    Service provider: design, develop and deliver the solution; manage data migration and validation; train
     users; provide go-live support.

 12. Assumptions
    Discounts apply to the list rate shown in the rate list in force on the invoice date.
    Not every customer has a discount on every product; the hierarchy in section 3.2 applies where no specific
     rule exists.
    Customer and discount data will be supplied in a structured format (Excel/CSV).
    All pricing is in a single currency (PKR).
    Override permission is controlled by user role and is not limited by percentage.

 14. Acceptance Criteria
    All masters are loaded correctly, with the items in section 7 resolved or marked Inactive.
    A sample of at least 5% of discount records matches the source data exactly.
    Test invoices pick up the correct percentage or fixed net price from the matrix.
    Changing a discount on an invoice, per line or for the whole invoice, changes only that invoice. The
     matrix value is identical before and after. This is tested on draft invoices, posted invoices and quote
     conversions.
    Reset to matrix, override labels and the override reason work as specified.
    Posted invoices are locked and unaffected by later matrix or rate changes.
